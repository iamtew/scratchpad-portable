MIXING PSYTRANCE

The other two notes are the machines.
This one is the mix: who owns which band, and where the saturation goes.

The kick and the bass are the engine.
Get those obvious with everything else muted.
Leads, hats, and delays decorate the engine. They do not get to move it.

==================================================
KICK
==================================================

Body and click are different jobs.

Body:
- sine sweep down into the sub
- a longer sine under it, so the downbeat is a thump and not a hole
- its own soft saturation, oversampled
- warm the kick here

The click is the top of that sweep, not a separate noise tick.
Start it nearer 100 Hz than 200 Hz.
Lowpass the kick after its saturator, or the new harmonics become the click.
If the click is the first thing you hear, the sweep is still too high.
The chest hit is the body.

Keep the kick short enough that the offbeat bass still fits after it.
Target is THUMP, not BOOOOM.

==================================================
BASS
==================================================

A filtered saw has almost no fundamental.
Saturating that only makes more fizz.

Order:

sine at the note
+ detuned saws
→ lowpass
→ waveshaper
→ a little EQ

The sine is what the shaper warms.
Oversample the shaper, or the new harmonics alias and the bass goes thin again.

A small cut around 250-450 Hz keeps the mud out.
Do not cut so hard that you scoop the warmth you just added.

Target is still TOK, not WUB.
If the bass already toks, leave it alone.

==================================================
LEADS
==================================================

Same hole, higher up.
A lone square is a whistle. It has no body, so it sounds thin beside the engine.

- saw, not square
- a quiet sine at the same note, into the same lowpass
- that sine is body in the mids, not a second bass in the sub
- a small saturator after the filter
- that saturator feeds the dry mix and the delay, so the echoes are not harsher than the note

Keep the cutoff moving.
The riff stays small. Full-on is da-da-da, not a hook.

Do not lowpass the lead down into the bass to make it "warm".
Bright and resonant is the point. Warmth is the fundamental plus a little saturation.

Lasers stay a fast pew into the delay.
They are not leads. Do not give them a body.

==================================================
TOP
==================================================

Hats and rides live above the lead.
Highpass them so they stay hats.

A high shelf on the mix, a few dB down around 7-8 kHz, takes the fizz off after the saturators.
Do not cut so hard that the hats disappear.

==================================================
DELAY
==================================================

Sync it to the tempo.
Highpass the return around 200-400 Hz.

Low notes repeating in the delay make the engine muddy.
Leads and zaps go to the delay.
The kick and the bass do not.

==================================================
SATURATION
==================================================

A little, in a few places.

- kick body
- bass
- lead
- one gentle stage on the whole sum

Oversample each one.
Drive none of them hard.

One master distortion, slammed by the kick, makes the bass and the lead pump.
The kick already has its own shaper.
The master stage only glues.

==================================================
MASTER
==================================================

Leave headroom.
The shaper curve clamps anything past full scale, and that clamp is the clip.
Turn the sum down before the glue, then makeup after it.
The level comes back. The flat top does not.
The destination clips hard too, and that also sounds thin.

No bus compressor at this tempo.
It pumps on every kick and the body disappears.

Volume sits after the glue, so mute and the end fade still work.

==================================================
CHECK
==================================================

Kick: thump, then a small tick. Not the other way around.
Bass: tok between the kicks.
Lead: warm enough to sit in the mids, and it loses a fight with the kick.
Highs are present and not in charge.
The loudest peak stays under full scale.
