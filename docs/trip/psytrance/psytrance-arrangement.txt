A PASS

One pass. Phrases of 16 bars. Then it ends.

Energy is a number from 0 to 100 on each phrase.
That number is how hard the phrase plays.

Tension is the last few bars of a phrase, and only when energy is already high.
A drop is low energy.

==================================================
WHO PLAYS
==================================================

Kick, and the offbeat bass: the engine. They run the track.
The roll opens only at the edge of a phrase.
That rule is in the agent note.

The engine is obvious before the squelch and the lead talk.
Acid and the lead sit out on a drop.

Hats sit out on a drop.
The ride waits until energy is high.
The hat body is in the mix note.

Pads breathe. They do not stay open.
The chord and that breath are in the mix note.

An ending can thin to kick and bass.
Then hold silence, so the delay and the reverb finish before the context closes.

==================================================
DO NOT
==================================================

Do not thin the bass for a phrase and leave it there.
Do not hide the pad in the noise bed.
Do not open the pad and leave it open. That rings.
