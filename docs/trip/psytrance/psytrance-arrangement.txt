THIS TRACK

144 BPM. One pass. Six phrases, 16 bars each, then an 8-bar outro.
Then 8 bars of kick and bass. Then 4 bars of silence, so the tails can finish.

The number on each phrase is energy, from 0 to 100.

1  15   intro
2  35   the roll, and the acid
3  55   the lead joins
4  85   climax
5  30   breakdown
6  90   final assault
7  90   outro, 8 bars
8  55   kick and bass, 8 bars
   --   silence, 4 bars

Tension is the last four bars of a phrase, and only when energy is already at 70.
A drop is energy at 30 or under. The intro and the breakdown are both drops.

==================================================
WHO PLAYS
==================================================

Kick, and the three-note bass roll: the whole track, and the last 8 bars alone.
The roll opens only at the edge of a phrase.
That rule is in the agent note.

Acid: the lead from phrase 2. Out for a drop, and out for the outro.
The squelch, the pattern, and the width are in the acid note.
The driver starts in the intro, stays through the breakdown, and stays for the outro.
It sits out the last 8.

Lead: from phrase 3. Out for a drop, and out for the outro.

Hats: not during a drop, and not for the last 8.
The ride waits until energy is at 70.
The hat body is in the mix note.

Pads: until the last 8.
They start almost silent and breathe across 32 bars, two phrases.
The chord and that breath are in the mix note.

==================================================
DO NOT
==================================================

Do not thin the bass for eight bars and leave it there.
Do not hide the pad in the noise bed.
Do not open the pad and leave it open. That rings.
